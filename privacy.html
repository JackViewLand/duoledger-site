DuoLedger Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20 · Home

Who uses this app

DuoLedger is a private tool used only by its two owners (a married couple). It is not offered to the public.

Google data the app accesses

  • Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file only.
  • The app can see and manage only the files and folder it creates itself in the owner's Google Drive: receipt images uploaded from Telegram. It cannot read any other Drive file.
  • A Google Sheets spreadsheet owned by the owner receives a read-only copy of the household transactions.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to record and report the owners' household expenses.
  • Transactions are stored in the owners' private database. Images are kept on the server only temporarily and are deleted after they are safely stored in Google Drive.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Google user data is not used to train AI models.

Deleting data

The owners can delete files in Google Drive at any time and can revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

隱私權政策（摘要）

DuoLedger 僅供擁有者夫妻兩人私人使用。只使用 drive.file 權限，只能存取本程式自己在雲端硬碟建立的收據圖片與資料夾，無法讀取其他檔案。資料僅用於記錄家庭收支，不會出售、分享給第三方或用於廣告。可隨時於 Google 帳戶撤銷存取權。

개인정보 처리방침 (요약)

DuoLedger 는 소유자 부부 두 사람만 사용하는 개인 도구입니다. drive.file 권한만 사용하며, 이 앱이 직접 만든 영수증 이미지와 폴더에만 접근할 수 있습니다. 데이터는 가계부 기록에만 사용되며 판매, 제3자 제공, 광고에 사용되지 않습니다. Google 계정에서 언제든지 접근 권한을 해제할 수 있습니다.
